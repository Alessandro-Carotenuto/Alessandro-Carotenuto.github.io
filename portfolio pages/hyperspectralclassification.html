Overview

Given a 15 × 15 hyperspectral image patch, CTA-Net predicts the land-cover class at its center using only a few labeled examples. This project reconstructs CTA-Net on Pavia University, where each class has 10 training and 5 validation pixels.

The model combines multiscale CNN features with a Conformer-style Transformer and channel-spatial attention. An experimental band-selection variant learns a global subset of spectral bands and exports a compact classifier.

Adaptive band selection

The model ranks bands, keeps one global subset, then fine-tunes a compact classifier. Default: 30% removed.

Key Elements

Few-shot protocol

10 labeled training pixels and 5 validation pixels per class.

CTA-Net backbone

Multiscale CNN, Conformer-style Transformer, and channel-spatial attention combine local and long-range features.

Sample amplification

Training-only noise, rotations, and same-class additions expand the few-shot data.

Adaptive band selection

Learns a global subset of spectral bands and fine-tunes a compact classifier.
